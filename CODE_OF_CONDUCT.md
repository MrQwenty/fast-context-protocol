# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Context Governance Protocol a respectful, harassment-free, technically rigorous experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, nationality, disability, appearance, religion, or personal circumstances.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and evidence;
- assume good faith while remaining precise about technical risks;
- welcome newcomers and explain project conventions constructively;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- protect private, personal, security-sensitive, and embargoed information;
- distinguish facts, hypotheses, opinions, and legal interpretations;
- accept maintainer decisions and use the documented governance process for disagreement;
- give credit for ideas, research, implementation, and documentation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- publishing private information without permission;
- deliberately disclosing vulnerabilities before coordinated disclosure;
- introducing secrets, personal data, customer data, or proprietary documents into public project spaces;
- impersonation, plagiarism, falsified benchmarks, or fabricated compliance claims;
- sustained disruption, spam, trolling, or bad-faith argument;
- retaliation against people who report concerns;
- using project spaces to promote hate, abuse, or illegal conduct.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, project chat, events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the lead maintainer through the contact methods listed in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md), or through GitHub's abuse-reporting mechanisms where appropriate.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, temporarily suspend access, permanently ban participants, or refer matters to platform operators or authorities when necessary.

Security vulnerability reports must follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement principles

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and protect reporters from unnecessary exposure. Maintainers involved in a complaint should recuse themselves when another qualified maintainer is available.

## Appeals

A participant may request reconsideration by providing relevant facts and acknowledging the impact of the behavior. Repeated arguments that merely restate the original dispute do not require further review.
