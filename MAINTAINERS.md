## Component ownership

Until additional maintainers are appointed, the lead maintainer owns all components.

Future maintainers should be listed with explicit ownership areas such as:

- protocol specifications and schemas;
- Go reference implementation;
- privacy gateway;
- security and threat model;
- conformance suite;
- website and documentation;
- SDKs and integrations;
- EU policy vocabulary and evidence formats.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are appointed according to [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md). Regular contribution alone does not automatically grant maintainer status; appointments require demonstrated technical judgment, reliability, and care for project safety.
